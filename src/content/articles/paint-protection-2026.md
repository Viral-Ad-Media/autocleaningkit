Washing and polishing make paint look good. Protection is what keeps it that way. A layer of wax, sealant or ceramic coating shields the clear coat from UV rays, bird droppings, tree sap and road grime, and it makes every future wash easier because dirt doesn't bond as tightly.

## Your options, from simplest to most durable

**Carnauba wax** gives a deep, warm glow that many enthusiasts love, especially on dark colors. It's easy to apply, but it typically lasts weeks rather than months.

**Synthetic sealants** are polymer-based. They bond to the paint more strongly than wax, last longer, and give a sharp, glassy shine.

**Ceramic sprays (SiO2)** bring some of the benefits of a ceramic coating in a spray-on, wipe-off product. They're slick, very water-repellent and generally outlast traditional waxes, and you can apply them after every few washes.

**Ceramic coatings** are the most durable option, lasting a year or more, but they need careful paint preparation and a slower, more exacting application. Many owners have them applied professionally.

**Spray detailers and gloss enhancers** are quick top-ups between washes. They boost gloss and slickness and remove light dust, but they aren't a substitute for a base layer of protection.

## What to look for

- **Durability** that matches how often you want to reapply
- **Ease of application.** Spray-on, wipe-off products are the most beginner-friendly.
- **Compatibility.** Most toppers can go over an existing sealant or coating; check the label.
- **Finish** you prefer: warm (wax) or glassy (sealant and ceramic)

## How to apply paint protection

1. **Wash and decontaminate** the paint with a clay bar first. Protection won't bond well to dirty paint.
2. **Polish** if needed. Protection locks in whatever's underneath, swirls included.
3. **Work in the shade** on a cool panel, one section at a time.
4. **Apply thin.** More product doesn't mean more protection; it just makes buffing harder.
5. **Follow the cure time** on the label before buffing, and keep the car dry for the time it recommends.

## Maintaining the finish

Wash with a pH-neutral shampoo so you don't strip the protection, and top it up with a spray sealant or ceramic detailer every month or two. When water stops beading tightly, it's time to reapply.
