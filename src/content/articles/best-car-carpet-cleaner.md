Car carpets trap everything: crumbs, sand, mud, coffee and the occasional mystery stain. Vacuuming handles the loose dirt, but ground-in grime and stains need a proper carpet cleaner. The right product and a few basic tools will get most carpets looking close to new.

## Types of car carpet cleaners

**Foaming spray cleaners** are the most common. The foam clings to the fibers, loosens dirt, and is easy to work in with a brush. They're good for general cleaning and light stains.

**Concentrated or all-purpose cleaners** can be diluted for different jobs and are good value if you clean often. Many also work on vinyl, rubber and leather.

**Enzyme and oxygen-based cleaners** break down organic stains such as food, drinks, pet accidents and sweat, and help remove the odors that come with them.

**Stain extractors** are formulated for spot treatment of stubborn marks rather than whole-carpet cleaning.

**Portable carpet shampooers and spot cleaners** spray cleaning solution into the fibers and then suck the dirty water back out. For heavily soiled interiors, extraction makes a dramatic difference that spray-and-wipe can't match.

## Tools that help

- A strong vacuum with a crevice tool, ideally a cordless handheld you'll actually use
- A stiff-bristled carpet brush or a drill brush attachment
- Microfiber towels for blotting
- A wet/dry vacuum or portable extractor for deep cleaning

## How to deep clean car carpets

1. **Remove the floor mats** and clean them separately.
2. **Vacuum thoroughly**, using a brush to loosen embedded dirt as you go. Move the seats forward and back to reach everything.
3. **Pre-treat stains** with a stain extractor and let it dwell for the time on the label.
4. **Spray the cleaner** on one section at a time; don't soak the carpet.
5. **Agitate** with a brush in two directions to work the cleaner into the fibers.
6. **Blot or extract** with a clean microfiber towel or an extractor until no more dirt transfers.
7. **Dry completely.** Leave the windows down or run the heater with the fan on high. Damp carpet leads to mildew and odors.

## Expert tips

- Always test a cleaner on a hidden spot first to check for color fastness.
- Blot stains, don't rub; rubbing spreads them and frays the fibers.
- Clean up spills as soon as possible. Fresh stains come out much more easily than old ones.
- Protect freshly cleaned carpets with good [floor mats](/best-car-carpets-2023).
