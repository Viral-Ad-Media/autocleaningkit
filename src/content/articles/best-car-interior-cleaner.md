A car's interior is a mix of materials that all want different care: textured plastic on the dashboard, glossy piano-black trim, fabric or leather seats, rubber seals and touchscreens. The best interior cleaner for you depends on which of those surfaces you have and how dirty they get.

## Types of interior cleaners

**All-purpose interior cleaners** are designed to be safe on most hard surfaces, including plastic, vinyl and rubber. They're the workhorse of interior detailing and handle everyday dust, fingerprints and spills.

**Interior detailers** are lighter quick-clean sprays that clean and leave a protective layer, often with UV protection to slow fading and cracking. Look for "matte" or "OEM finish" if you dislike shiny dashboards.

**Leather cleaners and conditioners** lift dirt from the grain and then replace the oils that keep leather supple. Use a dedicated product here; see our guide to [leather cleaners](/best-leather-cleaners-2026).

**Fabric and upholstery cleaners** foam into cloth seats to lift stains and odors, much like a [carpet cleaner](/best-car-carpet-cleaner).

**Screen and glass cleaners** are alcohol-free and ammonia-free formulas that won't damage the anti-glare coatings on infotainment screens or window tint.

## What to look for

- **Surface compatibility.** The label should list the materials it's safe on.
- **Finish.** Matte finishes reduce windshield glare; satin or gloss finishes look richer but attract dust.
- **UV protection** for dashboards and door tops that sit in the sun.
- **Low residue.** A good cleaner shouldn't leave a sticky or greasy film.
- **Scent.** Interior cleaners range from unscented to strongly perfumed. Pick one you'll be happy to live with.

## How to clean your car interior

1. **Declutter and vacuum** first, including vents, seat rails and door pockets.
2. **Dust with a soft brush** around vents, buttons and seams, with a vacuum nozzle nearby.
3. **Spray the cleaner onto a microfiber towel,** not directly onto the dashboard, so it doesn't drip into electronics or onto the windshield.
4. **Wipe in sections** and follow immediately with a dry towel.
5. **Clean the screens and glass last** with a dedicated cleaner and a fresh towel.
6. **Treat leather separately** with a cleaner and then a conditioner.

## Leather, fabric and dashboard care at a glance

| Surface | Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard and plastics | All-purpose interior cleaner, UV protectant | Silicone-heavy shine products, magic erasers |
| Fabric seats | Foaming upholstery cleaner, soft brush | Soaking the foam underneath |
| Leather seats | pH-balanced leather cleaner, then conditioner | Household all-purpose sprays, alcohol |
| Touchscreens | Screen-safe cleaner, soft microfiber | Glass cleaners with ammonia |
