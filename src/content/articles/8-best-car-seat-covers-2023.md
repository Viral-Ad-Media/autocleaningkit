Seat covers do two jobs: they protect the original upholstery from spills, pet hair, sun and wear, and they can refresh the look of an interior that has seen better days. The right set depends on how you use your car, whether that's kids in the back, a dog on the rear bench, or daily commuting in a hot climate.

## Universal versus custom-fit covers

**Universal covers** are designed to fit most bucket seats and benches. They're inexpensive and quick to install, but the fit is never perfect, and they can shift over time.

**Custom-fit covers** are patterned for a specific make, model and year. They look close to factory upholstery and stay in place, but cost considerably more.

## Common materials

- **Polyester and cloth** are affordable, breathable and easy to wash, and a good everyday choice.
- **Mesh** stays cooler in summer and is comfortable on long drives.
- **Neoprene** is water-resistant, so it's good for beach trips, gym bags and active lifestyles.
- **Faux leather (PU leather)** looks premium, wipes clean easily, and resists spills, but it can get hot in direct sun.

## Airbag safety

Many front seats have side airbags built into the seat bolster. If yours do, choose covers labeled as airbag-compatible, with tear-away stitching or cut-outs on the side. Never cover an airbag panel with a cover that isn't designed for it.

## What to look for

- A full set (front and rear) if you want consistent coverage, or front-only if you mainly need driver-seat protection
- Split-bench compatibility for rear seats that fold 60/40
- Openings for headrests, seat belts and child-seat anchors
- Machine-washable fabric, or wipe-clean faux leather
- Straps and hooks that secure the cover under the seat without damaging the trim

## Installation tips

Install covers on a warm day when the material is more flexible. Start with the headrest, pull the cover down evenly, and tuck the flaps deep into the seat crease before fastening the straps underneath. Take your time; a careful install is what separates a tidy fit from a baggy one.
